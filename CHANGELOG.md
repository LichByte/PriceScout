# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - first public release

- Price lookup: the cheapest NQ and HQ listing on every world in your data center or region.
- Prices on item tooltips, and "Check Prices" when you right-click an item.
- Popular items, watchlist with price alerts, price history chart, crafting profit, shopping list.
- Undercut checker that opens beside a retainer's sell list.
- Retainer sales log, including sales made while you were offline.
- Watchlist items can be set to HQ only.
- Crafting profit now prices ingredients by whole stacks on a single world, like the shopping list.
- Settings for how many listings and recent sales to load. The settings button in the plugin installer opens the Settings tab.
